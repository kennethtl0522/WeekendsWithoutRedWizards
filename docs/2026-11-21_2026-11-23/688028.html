Enjoy a peaceful couples getaway, or spend the weekend with the whole family at our house. The property is enclosed by trees for complete privacy, and features a large patio and a second story balcony. There is also a second driveway with room for an r.v. or two and a grill for a relaxing evening bbq. There is also a 2000 square foot barn, tables, and chairs available for larger gatherings. Pricing based on size of the gathering and time of year. The space The master bedroom features a Cal-King bed, a large t.v. a large shower, and a jetted bathtub. Two of the bedrooms have their own bathrooms/showers and two bedrooms share a jack & Jill bathroom with a tub and shower. Upstairs there are 2 Cal-King beds, and 4 full sized beds, with an extra Queen air mattress available for larger groups. All 8 beds available have sheets blankets and comforters and all but one of the bedrooms have walk-in closets available. We have cable in addition to standard dvd/blue ray players, and of course Wi-fi is included. We are located only 3.5 miles from the village of Arroyo Grande with lots of fun shops, restaurants, and wine-tasting rooms. Don't forget the Arroyo Grande Farmer's Market every Saturday from 12:00 noon to 2:30. We are located 7 miles from 7 world-class wineries: Talley, Laetitia, Wolff, Tangent, Edna Valley, Chamisal, Tolosa, etc. We are located only 6 miles from Lopez Lake and 6 miles from Pismo Beach. Lopez Lake has water slides for the kids and fishing and of course boating. Other things to note We set check in at 3pm and checkout at 11am. We do our best to accommodate early check in and late checkout requests however. Landscapers tend to the yard on Thursdays, please do not ask them to leave the premises.
