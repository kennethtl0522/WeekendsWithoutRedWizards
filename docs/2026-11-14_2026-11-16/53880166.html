Beautifully renovated and professionally designed four bedroom villa in Santa Barbara! The house is located only two houses from the beach and just a short walk from the Mesa Lane Steps beach access. Take a stroll in acres and acres of lush grounds right in your backyard with the Douglas Nature Preserve, BBQ burgers, chill at the hot tub or play ping pong- there's plenty for everyone. The 4 bedroom, 2 bathroom house sleeps up to 11 and comes well equipped for a relaxing beach vacation. The space 4 bedroom and 2 bathrooms with showers. Master bedroom has 1 king bed and an en-suite bathroom. One of the bedrooms have 2 twin beds, one has 2 double beds and one bedroom has 1 queen bed. House is about 1750 sq ft not including sunroom in the back with ping ping table. Garage fits 5 cars. Property is the 2nd house to Cliffs overlooking Ocean. There is Ocean access walking down steps, a 5 minute walk away from the house. A door in the backyard leads to the Douglas Nature Preserve, tens of acres of trails to hike with lush vegetation. Backyard also has hot tub, BBQ, outdoor furniture. New central heat pump with heating and cooling. Guest access Garage fits 3 cars inside and 3 on the outside. Street parking also available. Quiet residential neighborhood. No parties or loud events please. Please do not disturb neighbors. About 10 minutes to famous State Street and a couple of minutes to Cliff Street. Access to house is via digital keypad.
