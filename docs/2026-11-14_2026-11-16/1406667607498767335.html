Your Family’s Perfect Beach Getaway! Welcome to your home away from home! This spacious 4-bedroom, 3-bath beach house is designed with families in mind—featuring a fully equipped kitchen, cozy living spaces, and all the beach essentials you’ll need for fun in the sun. Wake up to stunning ocean views, relax on the deck with your morning coffee, and spend your days just steps from the sand. With room for everyone and plenty of amenities, it's the ideal spot to make lasting memories by the sea. The space This stunning home offers two separate family living areas — one downstairs and another upstairs — providing plenty of space and flexibility for relaxed family living or entertaining guests. Upstairs, you'll find three separate balconies, each capturing breathtaking ocean views. Step outside to experience true indoor-outdoor living. The expansive outdoor deck seamlessly connects to the interior through large frameless sliding glass doors, blending the spaces beautifully. Surrounding the home is lush, landscaped greenery that enhances the coastal ambiance. Enjoy evenings by one of the two outdoor fire pits, each with plenty of comfortable seating — the perfect setting for entertaining or unwinding under the stars. Guest access Guests have full access to the entire home, with the exception of the owner's closet upsatirs and down stairs and the cabinet doors in the garage, which will remain locked for storage.
