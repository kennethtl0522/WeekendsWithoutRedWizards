Wake up to the sound of the waves at this spacious oceanfront retreat located directly on the sand in beautiful Oceano, California. Perfect for families, friend getaways, and extended stays, this 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath beachfront home comfortably accommodates up to 12 guests and offers the ultimate California coast experience. The space Step outside and sink your toes into the sand — the beach is literally your backyard. Spend your mornings enjoying coffee on the oceanfront deck, afternoons exploring the famous Oceano Dunes, and evenings gathered around the firepit watching the sunset over the Pacific. Inside, you’ll find a bright and inviting living space designed for comfort and connection. The large living room is perfect for movie nights after a day at the beach, while the updated fully equipped kitchen makes it easy to prepare meals for the whole group. Enjoy indoor dining, fire up the propane BBQ on the deck, or explore the many nearby restaurants, wineries, and coastal attractions. Sleeping accommodations include: • 3 spacious bedrooms with luxury king beds • 1 bedroom with twin trundle beds • Queen sleeper sofa in the living room The oversized primary suite features a relaxing soaking tub, walk-in shower, and plenty of room to unwind after a beach day. One of the most unique features of this area is the famous Oceano Dunes State Recreation Area — the only California State Park where vehicles are permitted directly on the beach. Enjoy ATV riding, horseback riding, surfing, fishing, bird watching, and miles of scenic coastline right outside your door. Nearby attractions also include kayaking, wine tasting, cycling, shopping, dining, and more along the Central Coast. Property Highlights: • True beachfront location with direct beach access • Oceanfront deck with stunning views • Firepit & propane BBQ • Fully equipped kitchen • Washer & dryer • Central heating • Garage + driveway parking for up to 4 vehicles • Pet friendly (up to 2 pets with fee) • Free WiFi • Sleeps up to 12 guests Please Note: • This is a driving beach — 4WD/AWD vehicles are recommended for beach driving • No smoking • No air conditioning Whether you’re planning a relaxing coastal escape, a family vacation, or an unforgettable beach adventure, this Oceano beachfront home offers the perfect place to make lasting memories on California’s Central Coast. Guest access Guests have sole access to entire property during their stay.
